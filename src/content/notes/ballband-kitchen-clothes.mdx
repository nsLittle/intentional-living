  I love keeping my hands busy, even when I’m relaxing. Quiet evenings are often spent with an audiobook and a knitting project.

  More often than not, the project is the classic ballband dishcloth. I first found this pattern years ago on <a href="https://www.canadianliving.com/home-and-garden/knitting-and-crochet/article/knit-a-ballband-dishcloth" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Canadian Living</a>, and I’ve been making it ever since.  

  Always with sturdy cotton yarn (I use the ever-durable <a href="https://www.knitpicks.com/yarn/dishie/c/5420207" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dishie Yarn</a> from Knit Picks), chosen as much for its durability as for the pleasure of color. Over time, I’ve knit nearly every combination imaginable. At this point, I’ve made every color combination under the sun.

  My kitchen drawers are full of Dishie Clothes, and they get daily use from wiping counters, drying dishes, and generally making themselves indispensable.

  🧶 Knit up a few <a href="/crafts/ballband-kitchen-clothes" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dishie Clothes</a> for mindlessly practical knitting with this simple pattern.
