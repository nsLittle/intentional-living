text: |
  I was tromping through the woods in a section I rarely walk through, following no particular plan, when I came across a beautiful cluster of black truffles tucked beneath the leaf litter.

  They weren’t showy. No bright caps or upright stems — just dark, knotted forms half-hidden in the soil, easy to miss unless you slow down and really look. That felt fitting. Black truffles aren’t the kind of thing you stumble upon loudly. They reward wandering, curiosity, and a willingness to explore the edges of familiar paths.

  Finding them in an overlooked corner of the woods made the encounter feel quietly special, like a small secret the forest offered because I happened to be paying attention.
