  With a busy life, practicality reigns in the kitchen. While I love the romance of an all-day simmering sauce, the slow bubbling of a pot on the stove, the kitchen filled with warm cozy scents, most days call for something simpler, yet just as satisfying.

  That’s where my jars of go-to spice mixes come in. Every so often, my younger daughter and I set aside a Saturday morning to measure, mix, and stir together a few favorite blends — small preparations that support the rest of the week.

  It’s a simple ritual: spoonfuls of this, a quiet moment shared, and neat rows of jars by the time we’re done. 

  Keeping a small stash of pre-made blends changes the rhythm of our evenings. A sprinkle here, a pan warming on the stove, a simple side or two — and dinner arrives without much fuss.

  My Bold Earth spice mix is a family favorite — a jar of warm, vibrant spices inspired by the flavors of North Africa. Each sprinkle brings earthy heat, gentle smoke, and subtle sweetness, a blend that feels grounded, familiar, and quietly transportive.

  For an easy, reliable dinner, I sprinkle it over chicken thighs and whatever vegetables the season offers, then roast until tender. Served with rice, it becomes a meal that feels both grounding and complete.
