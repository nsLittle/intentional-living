---
title: "The Beauty of Symbiosis"
date: "2025-09-20"
hero: "/images/notes/lichen.png"
tags: ["woodland"]
text: |

  Lichens are beautiful examples of symbiotic living — two, and sometimes three, species forming a single, shared life.

  A fungus provides structure and protection, while algae or cyanobacteria contribute energy through photosynthesis. Neither could thrive quite the same way alone, yet together they create something resilient, adaptable, and unexpectedly lovely.

  I often notice lichens growing where conditions are less forgiving — on stone, bark, weathered wood — places that seem inhospitable at first glance. Their presence feels like a quiet lesson in cooperation, patience, and mutual reliance.

  Symbiosis isn’t loud or flashy. It doesn’t announce itself. It simply persists, shaping landscapes over time through steady, shared effort.
