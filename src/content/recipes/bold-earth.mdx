text: |-
  Bold Earth is a warm, balanced spice mix designed for everyday cooking. I keep a jar on hand for simple meals where I want depth of flavor without extra steps.

  It works especially well with chicken, roasted vegetables, and sheet-pan dinners. The blend brings earthy heat, gentle smoke, and subtle sweetness, making it easy to season a full meal with a single mix.

yield: "3/4 cup"
prep_time: "5 minutes"

ingredients:
  - "3 Tablespoons turmeric"
  - "2 Tablespoons cumin"
  - "2 Tablespoons smoked paprika"
  - "3 teaspoons coriander"
  - "3 teaspoons allspice"
  - "2 teaspoons cinnamon"
  - "2 teaspoons cayenne"
  - "3 teaspoons fine sea salt"
  - "3 teaspoons pepper"

steps:
  - "Measure and whisk all ingredients together."
  - "Transfer to a clean, dry jar. Label and date."
  - "Storage: cool, dark cupboard up to 6 months."

bakingPrepTime: "10 minutes"
bakingTime: "45 minutes"

bakingIngredients:
  - "1.5 pounds of chicken thigh with skin/bone (or your choice of protein)"
  - "1 large eggplant, cut into bite-sized cubes"
  - "2 Tablespoons of olive oil"
  - "2 Tablespoons of Bold Earth spice mix"

bakingSteps:
  - "Preheat oven to 425°."
  - "Toss eggplant cubes in 2 Tablespoons of olive oil until thoroughly coated; spread in an even layer on a sheet pan."
  - "Pat chicken thigh dry. Rub with remaining 2 Tablespoons of olive oil and 2 Tablespoons of Bold Earth spice mix."
  - "Nestle chicken thighs, with skin-side up, on top of the eggplant cubes."
  - "Bake for 20 minutes. The internal temperature of chicken should be 165 degrees or greater. Toss eggplants and rotate sheet pan."
  - "Continue baking for another 20–25 minutes until chicken skin is golden and eggplants are tender with browned edges."
  - "Enjoy with rice or other sides."
