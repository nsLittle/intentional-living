text: |-
  When winter feels long, my girls and I like to make something small to hang outside — a simple offering for the birds.

  These birdseed ornaments are easy to make, gentle for little hands, and sweet to see from the window on snowy mornings.

materials:
  - "¾ to 1 cup birdseed"
  - "1 packet unflavored gelatin (¼ oz)"
  - "¼ cup boiling water"
  - "Cookie cutters (or small silicone molds)"
  - "String or twine"
  - "Parchment paper or a baking sheet"
  - "A straw or skewer (to make a hole)"

instructions: |-
  1. **Prep your space.** Lay down parchment paper and set out cookie cutters or molds.
  2. **Dissolve the gelatin.** Pour boiling water into a bowl and whisk in the gelatin until fully dissolved.
  3. **Add the seed.** Stir in birdseed until everything is coated and the mixture thickens.
  4. **Shape your ornaments.** Spoon the mixture into cutters/molds and press down firmly.
  5. **Make a hanging hole.** Use a straw or skewer to create a small hole near the top of each ornament.
  6. **Let them dry.** Leave to set until firm (overnight is safest).
  7. **String and hang.** Thread twine through the hole and tie securely. Hang on a branch and enjoy the visitors.
