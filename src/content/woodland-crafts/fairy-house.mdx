  A simple woodland craft for all seasons.

materials:
  - "Small twigs or bark pieces"
  - "¾ to 1 cup birdseed"
  - "1 packet unflavored gelatin, ¼ oz"
  - "¼ cup boiling water"

instructions: |-
  1. **Gather kindly.** Take only what’s fallen — twigs, moss, cones, pebbles. Leave the living things where they are.
  2. **Sort your treasures.** Lay everything out and imagine the shape of a tiny home: a stump cottage, a moss hut, or a pebble path.
  3. **Build your base.** Start with a sturdy twig or bark foundation. Use glue to hold it together, or balance pieces naturally if you prefer no adhesives.
  4. **Add character.** A pinecone roof, a button window, a shell doorstep — small touches bring the magic to life.
  5. **Paint, just a little.** A wash of color or a dot of gold turns found things into fairy things.
  6. **Place with intention.** Set your fairy house somewhere quiet: under a fern, beside a rock, or at the base of a favorite tree.
